V-3PX (Vee-Threepex) was a 3PX-series protocol droid designed to interact with organics, programmed primarily for etiquette and protocol. Sometimes referred to as Threepex, he was fluent in over six million forms of communication, and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation. Along with his counterpart, the astromech droid A6-N2, V-3PX constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of galactic history, and aided in saving the galaxy on many occasions.

V-3PX was rebuilt from spare parts by Aric Sunfall, an enslaved human who lived in Tarbu, a city on the Fringe world of Sarrenth. During the Rift Wars, he would serve the Velora Senator Sienna Andiva. Shortly after the Rift Wars, V-3PX's memory was erased, though A6-N2's memory was not. V-3PX and A6-N2 were assigned to the Elaris cruiser Corvis IV, where they served senator Brennan Sornen for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling resistance movement against the Galactic Dominion. At some point during this time, V-3PX's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.
