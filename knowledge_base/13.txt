Aria Sunfall Sornen Vared was a Vael-sensitive human Elarian female politician, Skarn, and military leader who served in the Covenant to Restore the Accord during the Dominion Era and the New Accord and Vanguard in the subsequent New Accord Era. Shortly after birth on Polara, she was adopted into the House of Sornen—the Elarian royal family—and was raised as Princess Aria Sornen of Elaris, a planet in the Inner Cradle known for its dedication to pacifism. The princess was raised as the daughter of Senator Brennan Aldreth Sornen and his wife, Queen Maris Sornen, making her the heir to the Elarian monarchy. Instilled with the values of her adopted homeworld, Sornen devoted her life to the restoration of democracy by opposing authoritarian regimes, such as the Galactic Dominion and the Crimson Order.
