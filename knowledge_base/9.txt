Corin Velt, a human male native to Velhar, was a Dominion officer who served in the naval forces of the Galactic Dominion during the Crownfall War. At the time of the Battle of Glacis, he served as first officer of the Star Dreadnought Dominus—flagship of the Vohr Lord Vohrn Drael—assisting Admiral Vess Orlan. After Orlan committed a fatal mistake during the hunt for the Insurgent Covenant, Velt was promoted to admiral and given command of Drael's flagship. Velt served in that capacity until the Battle of Surnak, where he served as a Fleet Admiral, when the Dominion Navy confronted the Insurgent Fleet around the second Null Spire battle station in the year 4 ASR. The admiral perished aboard his starship when Arden Krynn's Razorwing starfighter crashed into the Dominus's bridge.
