Garron Vhal was a Tarnisan bureaucrat, military officer, and politician whose career spanned the waning years of the Galactic Accord into the Dominion Era. A member of Tarnis's prominent Vhal family, the man served the Accord's Judicial Department before returning to his homeworld to serve as its governor. When the Rift Wars began between the Accord and its splinter state, the Coalition of Free Worlds, Vhal enlisted in the Accord Navy. He served under Skarn General Oren Pell until his death on Vorlan Deep, where the two were held as prisoners by the Coalition for having memorized half of the Cendar Route. Saved by a Skarn-led rescue team, Vhal grew to respect Skarn Knight Aric Sunfall, who agreed with the captain's belief that the Skarn Code conflicted with their wartime duties.
