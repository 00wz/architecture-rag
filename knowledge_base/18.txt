A6-N2, pronounced Aysix-Entoo and often referred to as A6 (Aysix), was an A6-series astromech droid manufactured by Cybertech Forgeworks with masculine programming. A smart, spunky droid who served a multitude of leaders, A6-N2 was never given a full memory wipe nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in galactic history, his bravery and ingenuity saved the galaxy on numerous occasions.
