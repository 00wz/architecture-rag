Lirael Voss, also known affectionately as Double-V, was a human female Chandrosi politician and revolutionary leader who was one of the Covenant to Restore the Accord's founding members and its civilian leader. During the final decades of the Galactic Accord Era, Voss represented her homeworld of Chandros in the Accord Senate and, in this role, was an outspoken advocate of peace and democracy during the Rift Wars, a pan-galactic conflict between the Accord and its splinter state, the Coalition of Free Worlds. After the reorganization of the Accord into the Galactic Dominion at the end of the war, Voss retained her seat in the now-Dominion Senate, but she began organizing underground resistance movements to combat the Dominion government alongside militant Sorin Dax and fellow senator Brennan Sornen of Elaris. After years of leading a double life, Voss became the impetus for the official establishment of the Insurgent Covenant by resigning from the Senate, assuming leadership as Chancellor of the newly-established Insurgent Covenant. The Uprising, led by her, overthrew the Dominion in the Crownfall War and restored the Old Accord as the nascent New Accord, of which Voss was its first Chancellor.
