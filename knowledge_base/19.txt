Joss Varek, known as Talon SS-2-5 during his time at Ironvault Academy, was a renowned human male starfighter pilot from Corvann who first served in the Dominion Navy as a Talon fighter pilot until defecting to join the rebellion. He would also serve in the Covenant to Restore the Accord and years later the New Accord during their fight against the Galactic Dominion. He became a member of the Insurgent Covenant during the Crownfall War and fought in several battles of the war. He fought in the Battle of Calon, where he flew in Sable Squadron alongside Joren Sunfall. After Sunfall destroyed the Null Spire, he and Varek were left as the only survivors of Sable Squadron.
