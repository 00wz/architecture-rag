Oneth was a legendary Skarn Grand Master who led the Skarn Order through the time of the High Accord, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Vohr, and during the transformation of the Galactic Accord into the Galactic Dominion. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Oneth trained generations of Skarn, ultimately serving as the Skarn Order's Grand Master. He played integral roles in defending the Accord during the Rift Wars, survived Protocol Zero, and lived to pass on the Skarn tradition to Joren Sunfall, and unlocking the path to immortality.
