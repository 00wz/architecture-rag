Vextra, a human female legionnaire captain of the Crimson Order, grew up as a member of the Veyr clan on the nuclear-ravaged world of Veldross, where she became a formidable warrior, eventually becoming the clan's military leader. Seeking passage offworld, Vextra helped the Crimson Order General Harlan Verux find his ship, in the process betraying her own brother, Verro, and the rest of her clan. After being rescued, Vextra joined the Crimson Order, abandoning her former life.

Over the years, Vextra rose through the ranks of the Crimson Order, eventually becoming the leader of their legionnaire training program. She conspired with Harlan's son, General Galen Verux, to assassinate her former benefactor, resulting in Harlan's former protege, Sentry, attempting to kill her after the assassination was carried out. Having consolidated her position within the Crimson Order, Vextra became part of an unofficial triumvirate consisting of Galen and the dark warlord of the Crimson Order, Zarek Vohl.
