Gungo Plonk was a male Ottek Yssan military commander and politician who played a key role during the Invasion of Velora and the Rift Wars that culminated in the fall of the Galactic Accord and the rise of the Galactic Dominion. Once an outcast from Yssan society due to his clumsy behavior, he regained favor with his people by helping secure an alliance between the Yssan boss Rugan Mok and Queen Sienna Andiva of Velora, an alliance vital in ending the Mercantile Union's invasion of their shared homeworld. In the years that followed, Plonk became a Junior Representative for his people in the Stellar Senate, serving alongside Andiva once she became the planet's senator.
