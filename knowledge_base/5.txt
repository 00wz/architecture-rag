Vesco Drayce was a human male crime lord and former bounty hunter whose career spanned decades, from the fall of the Galactic Accord to the end of the rule of the Galactic Dominion. Originally code-named Cipher, he was an unaltered clone of the famed Korthan bounty hunter, Karro Drayce, who raised the boy as his son. Vesco emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of Korthan armor. His personal starship was the Hex-1, a Skyhawk-31-class patrol and attack craft that once belonged to Karro. Trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, Drayce was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy during the reign of Emperor Castavor. He became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the Dominion and the extensive criminal underworld. After living among the Dune Stalkers of Sarrenth for a time, he took over as Suzerain of Tarbu during the era of the New Accord.
