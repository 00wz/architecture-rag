Korin Zushu, known only as Zushu until being formally adopted by Korin Valtaar after the Liberation of Korthas, was a male Vael-sensitive Korthan belonging to the same mysterious species as the legendary Grand Master Oneth. Zushu was born around the year 41 BSR, and was raised at the Skarn Temple on Tellaris as a Skarn Initiate of the Skarn Order. Zushu had survived the Great Skarn Purge and was hidden away. Years later, he was rediscovered by a remnant of the Galactic Dominion after it fell and led by Varl Saren. At this point, Zushu became known to many simply as the Whelp, during the New Accord Era. However, Zushu was rescued by Korin Valtaar, a Korthan bounty hunter where he became a foundling. Sharing many adventures together, Valtaar was eventually able to find a Skarn to train Zushu and he rejoined the Skarn Order under Skarn Master Joren Sunfall. Later on though, Sunfall sensed that Zushu's heart was not in learning the Skarn path, thus he subsequently gave in to his attachment to the Korthan and was returned to him upon which he would start to train to become a Korthan himself.

With the beginning of the Skarn Purge and dawn of the Dominion Era, he was taken into hiding by Skarn Master Talven Rho. Eventually, he was held at a hideout run by Krevni mercenaries on Hadron-7 during the era of the New Accord, having survived the siege of the Skarn Temple decades prior. While still an infant at the age of fifty years, he was sought by the "the Patron," on behalf of Varl Saren and Doctor Edran Vossk, by a Dominion client who hired Valtaar to retrieve him.
