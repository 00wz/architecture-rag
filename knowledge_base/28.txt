Corvan Dross, a Vael-sensitive human male, was a venerable if maverick Skarn Master who lived during the last years of the Accord Era. He was a wise and well-respected member of the Skarn Order, and was offered a seat on the Skarn Council, but chose to reject and follow his own path. Adhering to a philosophy centered around the Living Vael, Dross strove to follow the will of the Vael even when his actions conflicted with the wishes of the High Council. After encountering Aric Sunfall, Dross brought him to the Skarn Temple on Tellaris, convinced he had found the Promised One. His dying wish was for Sunfall to become a Skarn and ultimately restore balance to the Vael.
