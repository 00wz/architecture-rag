Brennan Aldreth Sornen of the planet Elaris was a human male Elarian politician, statesman, and revolutionary leader who championed democratic and progressively-minded causes during the decades he represented his homeworld in the Senates of the Galactic Accord and the Galactic Dominion. Born Brennan Aldreth, the once-adventurous young man forsook his familial legacy to marry Maris Sornen, Elaris's queen. Together, the two fought to keep Elaris strong, with Brennan being elected to the Stellar Senate after the Invasion of Velora in 32 BSR. In the Senate, he built a reputation through his commitment to honesty and justice, forming ties with like-minded senators such as Lirael Voss of Chandros, Ondaco Marr of Vossa, and Sienna Andiva of Velora and becoming the Skarn Order's best ally in the Senate. Sornen and his faction worked tirelessly to keep the Accord's democratic values alive amid the burgeoning Secessionist Crisis.
