Aric Sunfall was a legendary Vael-sensitive human male who was a Skarn Knight of the Galactic Accord and the prophesied Promised One of the Skarn Order, destined to bring balance to the Vael. Also known as "Ari" during his childhood, Sunfall earned the moniker "Fearless Blade" from his accomplishments in the Rift Wars. His alter ego, Vohrn Drael, the Dark Lord of the Vohr, was created when Sunfall turned to the Umbral side of the Vael, pledging his allegiance to the Vohr Lord Vohrn Maledict at the end of the Accord Era.

A vergence in the Vael, Aric Sunfall was born on the desert planet of Sarrenth in the Fringe Reaches in 41 BSR. He was the son of Shara Sunfall, a slave who conceived a child without a father. His blood contained over twenty-thousand aurivites, surpassing Grand Master Oneth and all other Skarn in the galaxy. Corvan Dross, the Skarn Master who discovered Sunfall during the Invasion of Velora in 32 BSR, theorized that Sunfall was conceived by the aurivites. Following the Battle of Velora, the Skarn High Council admitted Aric Sunfall into the Skarn Order as the Aspirant of Skarn Knight Oberon Vance despite feeling that he was too old and emotional to adhere to the Skarn Code. Sunfall remained an Aspirant through the beginning of the Rift Wars, instigated by events set in motion by Count Vorlen. Although Skarn doctrine prohibited romantic relationships, Aric Sunfall had a secret wife, Senator Sienna Andiva of Velora. During the early days of the Rift Wars, Sunfall served as a Skarn General in the Grand Army of the Accord, commanding the legion troopers of the elite Ashen Legion against the Coalition of Free Worlds. After receiving his knighthood, Sunfall oversaw the Skarn training of his own apprentice, Ashka Veno. By 19 BSR, when Veno walked away from the Skarn Order, Sunfall began to struggle with feelings of failure towards his apprentice and the Skarn Order itself.
