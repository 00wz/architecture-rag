Sienna Andiva Tarro, also simply known as Sienna Andiva, was a human female politician who represented the people of Velora during the final years of the Galactic Accord. Prior to her career as a senator in the Stellar Senate, Andiva was the elected ruler of the Royal House of Velora. A political idealist, she advocated for the preservation of democracy as well as a peaceful resolution to the Rift Wars. However, her secret marriage to the Skarn Knight Aric Sunfall would have a lasting effect on the future of the galaxy for decades to come.

Born in 46 BSR during the Age of the Accord, Sienna Tarro was raised by her parents, Ruwen Tarro and his wife Jara, on the peaceful Median Belt world of Velora. Despite the humble background of the Tarro family, Sienna was identified early for her potential in public service. Devoting her life to civic duty, she was elected queen and, therefore, adopted the regnal name "Andiva" in 32 BSR. Due to the machinations of her advisor, Senator Veylin Castavor, Andiva's reign was put to the test by the Mercantile Union's blockade and subsequent invasion of Velora. Unable to fight the Federation's army of battle droids without assistance, the queen beseeched the Stellar Senate on Tellaris for help, only to see her efforts hampered by bureaucracy and procedure. Although she ultimately liberated her homeworld with the help of the Skarn Order and the Yssan Grand Army, her decision to cast a Vote of No Confidence against Fennic Valoran enabled Castavor to become the Supreme Chancellor.
