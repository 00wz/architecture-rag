Vohrn Maledict, born Veylin Castavor and also simply known as the Emperor, was a male human Dark Lord of the Vohr and Emperor of the Galactic Dominion, with his reign lasting from 19 BSR to 4 ASR. Rising to power in the Stellar Senate as the senator of Velora, the secretive Vohr Lord cultivated two identities, Maledict and Castavor, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal. He orchestrated the fall of the Galactic Accord and the Skarn Order through the Rift Wars, and then established his reign over the galaxy which lasted until his death at the Battle of Surnak. However, the Umbral side of the Vael gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death, seeking further immortality until the Emperor was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the Battle of Vaxhold by Sora, his granddaughter born from his Vat-grown clone son Daven and his daughter-in-law Mirae.
