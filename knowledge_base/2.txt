Drubo, also referred to as Drubo the Vorlax, and nicknamed "Vundo Marfin" (translated as Squishy Bun) by his father and "Whiffy" by Ashka Veno, was a male Vorlax and the son of Jorba the Vorlax. During the Rift Wars, he was secretly kidnapped by the Coalition of Free Worlds, which was orchestrated by Count Vorlen with help from Zevro, Drubo's great-uncle. The plot was meant to discredit the Skarn Order and scuttle negotiations between the Galactic Accord and the Vorlaxes, but the Skarn were able to rescue him. He was kidnapped by Ravenn Vexis and taken to the planet Varn. A Skarn rescue team consisting of Aric Sunfall and his Aspirant Ashka Veno traveled to Varn to rescue him. He then became sick and nearly died on his way to Sarrenth but survived thanks to Ashka Veno. After Aric Sunfall and Veno evaded Vorlen, who tried to kill Drubo and blame the Skarn, Drubo was returned to his father. Drubo was later present when Baron Notavar Papandros visited Jorba's Palace. In 20 BSR, Vexis returned to Sarrenth, and wished to avoid attention from Jorba as she had previously kidnapped his son. Drubo lived past the Dominion Era and into the New Accord Era, becoming a gladiator in the fighting pits of Zhao.
