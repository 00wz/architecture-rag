Colonel Thorne was a human individual who served within the Covenant to Restore the Accord and later in the New Accord as a starfighter pilot during the New Accord Era. During that time, she contacted the Korthan bounty hunter Korin Valtaar on Calder Base and sent him on a mission on the Fringe to take out both Dominion Remnant forces and gangsters of the Vorlax Clan.
