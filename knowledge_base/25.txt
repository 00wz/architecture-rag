Maraxus, born Qhemar jai Shellan, was a Drakell male warlord who served within the military forces of the Coalition of Free Worlds as a commanding officer during the Rift Wars in the final years of the Accord Era. In addition to his position as a general within the Secessionist Droid Army, the Drakell held the title of Supreme Martial Commander of the Secessionist Droid Armies throughout the war, where he led countless engagements against the Accord's Grand Army. Recognized for his ruthless tactics and extensive cybernetic enhancements, Maraxus utilized the Secessionist Droid Army and his adept combat skills to instill fear throughout the galaxy as he traveled to and invaded planets while engaging numerous Knights of the Skarn Order, his sworn enemies.
