Lorin Bavrandom Castrel, otherwise known as Lorin Castrel, was a human smuggler, gambler, and entrepreneur who became Baron Administrator of Skyhaven and, later, a general in the Insurgent Covenant. Born on Salvoor, he was the owner of the Nova Drifter before losing it to Jaro Vared in a game of drazil on Numara Prime. Afterward, Castrel put an end to his days as a smuggler and became an entrepreneur, setting up a small mining operation on the planet Renvar before eventually becoming the leader of Skyhaven in the skies of the planet Aerix.

During the Crownfall War, Vohrn Drael arrived on Skyhaven to lay a trap for his son, Joren Sunfall. As part of the trap, the Dark Lord of the Vohr forced Castrel into tricking a group of Insurgents, including Vared and Princess Aria Sornen, leading them to Drael himself. Though Drael promised to leave Skyhaven without a Dominion presence, Castrel felt the deal had been altered to the point where he could no longer tolerate it. Castrel alerted his citizens to the Galactic Dominion's presence and impending occupation and ordered an evacuation. He helped the Insurgents try to rescue Vared, who had been frozen in cryonite, from Vesco Drayce, but the bounty hunter escaped.
