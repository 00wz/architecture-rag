Maren Ackvor was a male Caltari veteran soldier and prominent revolutionary leader during the Rift Wars, the Crownfall War, the Cold War, and the war between the Vanguard and the Crimson Order. Throughout his sixty years of service, Ackvor was regarded as a brilliant tactician.

Ackvor hailed from the planet Caltara and served as the captain of the Caltari Guard. During the Rift Wars, Ackvor fought in the Battle of Caltara, in which he helped to secure Prince Sel-Mar's ascent as King of Caltara and repel the Coalition of Free Worlds. After the rise of the Galactic Dominion, Ackvor became the foremost military commander in the Covenant to Restore the Accord, emerging as a symbol of defiance against the Dominion's subjugation of non-humans. His presence attracted Caltari support for the Covenant, thus supplying the Caltari star cruisers that made up a bulk of the Covenant Fleet.
