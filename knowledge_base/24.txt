Veyd was a Vael-sensitive Vethmoorian Veshk male who was apprenticed to the Vohr Lord Vohrn Maledict, and after his defeat by the Skarn Oberon Vance, he established himself as a crime lord during the Rift Wars and the Dominion Era. The renegade Vohr Lord was a formidable warrior, driven by hatred against the Skarn and his former Vohr Master.

Born in 54 BSR during the time of the Galactic Accord, Veyd was a son of Mother Velhaste, who led the Nightweavers of Vethmoor. Taken as a child by Vohrn Maledict, Vohrn Veyd was molded into a deadly Dark Lord of the Vohr, growing in hatred of the Skarn Knights who served the Accord and the Lumen side of the Vael—while the Skarn Order had deemed the Vohr extinct for a thousand years, Veyd's power-hungry master set his Grand Plan into motion with the Mercantile Union's Invasion of Velora in 32 BSR. Vohrn Veyd announced the return of the Vohr on Sarrenth, where he tried to kill the Skarn emissaries of the Accord's Supreme Chancellor. However, Veyd's failure to do so allowed Skarn Master Corvan Dross and his pupil, Aspirant Oberon Vance, to bring Queen Andiva of Velora and a boy strong in the Vael—exceptionally so, to the extent that Dross believed this "Aric Sunfall" to be the prophesied Promised One who would bring balance to the galaxy—to Tellaris, the heart of the Accord.
