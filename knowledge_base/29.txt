Tovan Reyes was a revered Vael-sensitive human male Skarn Master and member of the Skarn High Council during the last years of the Accord Era. During his time in the Skarn Order, he once served as an elected leader of the Skarn and, during the Rift Wars, as a Skarn General in The Grand Army of the Accord. He was the greatest champion of The Skarn Order and promoted its ancient traditions amidst the growing influence of the Umbral side of the Vael in the corrupt, declining days of the Accord.
