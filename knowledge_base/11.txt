Jorba Deshaliik Tor, more commonly referred to as Jorba the Vorlax or simply Jorba, and formally styled as His Excellency Jorba Deshaliik Tor of Vorrun, Eminence of Sarrenth, was a Vorlax male gangster and crime lord, as well as a leading member of the Grand Vorlax Council and influential leader of the Vorlax Clan, who operated and led a criminal empire from his palace on the Fringe world of Sarrenth. Jorba was a major figure on Sarrenth, where he controlled the bulk of the piracy, slavery, and trafficking in illegal goods that generated most of the planet's wealth. He was also highly influential in the entire Fringe as one of its most powerful crime lords.

During the Rift Wars, Jorba's influence and power over the Fringe, specifically its warp-lanes, was sought by both the Galactic Accord and the Coalition of Free Worlds, who both courted Jorba's approval. When Drubo, Jorba's son, was kidnapped, the crime lord promised his support to whoever returned his son. When the Secessionists were revealed to have orchestrated Drubo's kidnapping, Jorba pledged his support to the Accord.
