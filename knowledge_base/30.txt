DJ-8, sometimes spelled and pronounced Deejay-Ate and nicknamed DJ, was a DJ-series astromech droid who operated approximately thirty years after the Battle of Surnak. He had a dome head, similar to that of A6-series astromech droids, with the bulk of his body made up of a ball on which the droid's head rolled. DJ-8 was mostly white, with some silver and orange on his body, as well as a black photoreceptor. The droid belonged to Vanguard pilot Renn Dovar, whom he accompanied during his flights on his T-70 Starwing starfighter.
