Oberon Vance was a legendary Vael-sensitive human male Skarn Master who served on the Skarn High Council during the final years of the Accord Era. As a High Skarn General, Vance served in the Grand Army of the Accord that fought against the Secessionist Droid Army during the Rift Wars, and was nicknamed "the Diplomat." Vance, however, was forced into exile as a result of the Great Skarn Purge initiated by the Accord's own legion troopers when Accord Supreme Chancellor, secretly Vohrn Maledict, implemented Protocol Zero. As a mentor, Vance was responsible for training two members of the Sunfall family, Aric and Joren Sunfall, both of whom served in turn as his Aspirant in the ways of the Vael.

Vance was born on the planet Brevon in 57 BSR. He was Vael-sensitive, and therefore taken from his family and inducted into the Skarn Order as a child. Raised in the Skarn Grand Temple on the planet Tellaris, Vance was assigned to Skarn Master Corvan Dross. In 32 BSR, Vance attained the rank of Skarn Knight by defeating the Vohr Lord Vohrn Veyd during the Invasion of Velora. He also chose to train Aric Sunfall—the prophesied Promised One who was destined to destroy the Vohr and restore balance to the Vael—having promised Dross who died in the plasma glaive duel against Veyd.
